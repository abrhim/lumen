# Stick of Joseph — ingest research

Channel research, 2026-08-18, verified against YouTube's own page data (full
239-item podcast playlist enumerated) and thestickofjoseph.com. Feeds the
`shows/stick-of-joseph.mjs` config and the collection split.

## Two name corrections that gate everything

- **"Todd McLauchlin"** — every video title spells it McLauchlin. Only the
  channel's own playlist title misspells it "McLaughlin". A title match on
  "McLaughlin" finds ZERO episodes. Match both spellings defensively.
- **"Andrea Woodmansee"** — not "woodmansea". Early episodes credit her only
  as "Andrea W."; the Wisemen episode confirms the surname on-channel. Her
  "Hidden Hebrew Wedding Ritual and The Temple" (dU81hfwml6Q) is the most
  viewed video on the whole channel at 382K.

## The channel

86.2K subscribers, 303 videos, ~11.17M views, running since early 2023,
roughly weekly. After removing duplicates and sub-15-minute clips: **~180
unique full-length episodes**, median 69 minutes, 52 over 90 minutes, the two
longest just under 5 hours. Hosts: Chad Heath, Dave Butler (also runs the
Plain & Precious Publishing imprint), Dr. Mike Day. No members-only videos —
monetisation is Patreon/DonorBox, which is what the ad-free re-uploads are for.

## DECIDED (Abram, 2026-08-18): five collections, no Interviews catch-all

The main-interviews collection (~120 episodes) is deferred — "we can do
something for those later." The ingest scope is exactly the members of the
five named collections, ~55-58 unique episodes. Claim priority when an
episode could sit in two: Todd > Andrea > Mike & Dave > Stick of Judah >
Live events. With verified canonical IDs in hand, discovery feeds EXPLICIT
episode lists rather than a channel scan — which also sidesteps most of the
ad-free-duplicate hazard.

## Ingest hazards, in order of danger

1. **Ad-free duplicate uploads — the big one.** ~67 of 239 interview-playlist
   items are public "AD Free" re-uploads, sometimes RETITLED, plus ~16 silent
   re-uploads with no marker at all (verified pairs: aWU9098rwQ4=R7zabyN0ZOg,
   bnYMHtwZP0I=SWqAiMcwwBs; Todd's "Knowing God" exists three times, all
   1:30:33). Title matching cannot dedupe. Use duration-equality plus
   view-count ratio, or an explicit blocklist. The Unshaken pipeline has
   never faced this hazard class.
2. **Livestream dead air.** Launch events are 2+ hour streams with waiting
   rooms. Where a trimmed cut exists (Smoot: oNV0gr7VT_c 39:15 vs
   won1FoY03sg full stream), prefer the trimmed cut.
3. **Diarization.** Standard episodes are 2–3 speakers. Launch panels are
   4+. The validation run must include one 3-speaker episode.
4. **The 15-minute clip cutoff has edge cases.** Holy Week Day 2 is 16:05
   (barely in, correctly). "An Enemy Hath Done This Pt. 1" is 13:58 and is a
   REAL episode the cutoff would wrongly drop — whitelist if that series
   matters.
5. **Collab cross-posts** (High Low Brow, By Small & Simple Screens) may
   exist as duplicates on partner channels. Harmless here.
